LOQmusic: free music streaming, no sign-up, no ads

LOQmusic is a free music streaming web app. Search and play any song, album, artist or playlist right in your browser, on desktop, tablet or phone. There's no account to make and no ads to sit through.

Open LOQmusic and start listening

Features

  • Search and stream any song, album, artist or playlist
  • Word-synced lyrics from 12 providers, with translation
  • AutoMix: beat-matched DJ transitions, plus crossfade
  • 10-band equalizer with 14 presets, playback speed and a sleep timer
  • 16 genre and mood radio stations and a personal station
  • Listen Together: hear the same song at the same moment as your friends
  • Liked songs, playlists, saved albums, followed artists and history, kept in your browser

Questions

Is LOQmusic free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no sign-up, and nothing to install.

Do I need an account?

No. LOQmusic only asks for a name to greet you by. Your library stays in your own browser.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.

Who made LOQmusic?

LOQmusic is a non-commercial portfolio project by developer Chandan Pal (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram).
